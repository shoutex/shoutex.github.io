What InSEO checks

Most SEO tools give you surface-level stats. InSEO starts from the keyphrase you want the page to rank for and checks every element against it.

TTitle and meta

Is the keyphrase in the title and meta description, and are they the right length?

HHeadings

Does the H1 carry the keyphrase, and do H2 to H5 headings structure the page?

LLinks

Do internal links use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text, and which external domains do you cite?

KKeyphrase in the body

How often each keyword appears in the body copy, so you can spot thin or stuffed pages.

OGOpen Graph tags

Are the tags that control link previews on social and chat apps present?

UURL

Is the URL short, readable and built around the topic?

IMGImages

Are images present and described with alt text?

%One diagnostic score

Every check rolls into one on-page score, so you see how far a page is from fully optimized.
