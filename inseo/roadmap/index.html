Where is InSEO going next?

InSEO checks classic on-page SEO today. Next, it will help pages perform in AI-driven search as well. Here is the plan, from what is live to what we are still exploring.

Live nowKeyphrase on-page scoring

Up to four keywords and synonyms, scored across titles, meta, OG tags, URL, headings, links, images and body copy.

Planned beta · 2027AI Insights

An AI layer that explains the score and suggests specific improvements for the page and keyphrase in front of you.

Planned beta · 2027AI search readiness and semantic suggestions

Guidance on how well a page answers in AI-driven results, plus related terms and topics the page should cover.

ExploringLocal SEO signals

Checks for businesses that sell locally, such as location in titles and headings, business details and local schema.

What will AI Insights look like?

Today the AI Insights tab is a placeholder. When you click it in the beta, InSEO will send the page's scores to an AI back end and return a short, specific read on how well the page is doing and what to change first. The mock-up below shows the direction, not a final design.

How could InSEO help with local SEO?

Many of the pages people optimize are location pages: a clinic in Toronto, a law firm in Calgary, a service area page. The checks we are exploring would score how clearly a page signals its location: the place in the title and H1, consistent business name, address and phone, local business schema and links to nearby location pages.

These checks complement a full local audit rather than replace it. Our guide to running a local SEO audit covers the profile, reviews and citation work that sits outside any single page.

Hint

If you manage location pages and want local checks in the beta, say so when you sign up. Real use cases decide what we build first.
