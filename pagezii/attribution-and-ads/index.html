Phases 3 and 4: attribution and conversational ads

Visibility matters only if it leads somewhere. Phase 3 connects AI discovery to website visits and conversions with the signals that exist today. Phase 4 adds a research module for ads that appear inside AI answers.

ShoutEx Team · Updated October 2026 · Page 4 of 4
Pagezii roadmap / Phases 3 and 4: Attribution and ads

Which signals show AI-driven visits today?

Three signals are available now. Referral traffic from AI apps, which ChatGPT makes easier by adding a UTM source; OpenAI's publishers and developers FAQ confirms that ChatGPT appends utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral links. Server logs showing AI crawlers and user-triggered fetchers. And the visibility data from Phase 1, which can be joined to both by date and page.

How should AI referrals be tracked in GA4?

Create a custom channel for AI assistants so these sessions stop disappearing into Referral. GA4's custom channel groups let you define a channel with a source regular expression and place it above Referral in the evaluation order.

ga4Channel name:  AI assistants
Condition:     Session source matches regex
Regex:         ^(chatgpt\.com|chat\.openai\.com|perplexity\.ai|.*\.perplexity\.ai|gemini\.google\.com|copilot\.microsoft\.com|claude\.ai)$
Order:         above Referral

Our guide on tracking AI search traffic covers the full GA4 setup, including reports and the referrers that change most often.

What can server logs add?

Logs show which pages AI systems fetch, which is the closest available signal to what they read. OpenAI documents its crawlers and user agents, separating training crawls from search indexing and from fetches triggered by a user's question. Google lists its common crawlers, including the Google-Extended token that controls use for AI models.

pythonimport re
from collections import Counter

AI_AGENTS = re.compile(r"(GPTBot|OAI-SearchBot|ChatGPT-User|PerplexityBot|Perplexity-User)")
LINE = re.compile(r'"(?:GET|HEAD) (?P<path>\S+) [^"]*" \d{3} \S+ "[^"]*" "(?P<ua>[^"]*)"')

def ai_fetches(log_lines):
    hits = Counter()
    for line in log_lines:
        m = LINE.search(line)
        if m and (agent := AI_AGENTS.search(m["ua"])):
            hits[(agent.group(1), m["path"])] += 1
    return hits   # join with citations.url from Phase 1 by path
Important

User agents can be spoofed. Verify important crawler traffic against the IP ranges each company publishes before using it in a customer report.

How do the three signals come together?

Join on page and week, not on the user. A useful first report shows, for each page: how often Phase 1 saw it cited, how often AI agents fetched it and how many AI-referred sessions and conversions it received. Read it as correlation and say so. Much AI influence is invisible: a buyer reads an answer, then types the brand into Google a day later. Pagezii's note on where answer engine optimization and SEO differ explains why last-click models undercount this channel.

CTO rule

Report the gaps next to the numbers.

Cross-AI attribution will never be complete. A report that names what it cannot see earns more trust than one that pretends to see everything.

How should Phase 4 study conversational ads?

Treat ads inside AI answers as a research module on top of Phases 1 to 3. OpenAI has described its approach to ad formats and measurement in ChatGPT, which gives the first concrete surface to study. The questions for Pagezii are whether ad exposure changes organic mention rates for the same prompts, and whether AI-referred conversions differ between paid and organic placements.

Start with logging: record when an ad appears in a collected answer, its position relative to organic recommendations and the advertiser. Our guide on measuring leads and pipeline from ChatGPT ads covers the advertiser side of the same measurement problem.

Key point

Phase 4 is research, not product. Ship it as a method and a published note first; build software only when the method holds up.
